Proposal #482 · Document capture · 12 source chats

"My document keeps getting rejected even though it looks fine"

Medium confidence
Document rejections most often come from glare or partial edges, not blur. Try placing your ID on a flat, dark surface (a table or magazine works well), turn off your flash, and make sure all four corners are inside the frame before tapping the capture button. If that doesn't help, try moving to a window with indirect natural light.
Source: GitBook / Document capture troubleshooting · Verification Issue KB / Edge detection failures
Proposal #481 · Browser · 8 source chats

"Camera won't open when I tap on the verification link from WhatsApp"

High confidence
WhatsApp opens links in its in-app browser, which blocks camera access for security reasons. Tap the three dots in the top corner of your WhatsApp browser and choose "Open in browser", or copy the link and paste it into Chrome or Safari. The verification will work normally there.
Source: Verification Issue KB / In-app browser limitations
