Anatomy & motion

One object that moves.

The rail
Glass surface, 16 margin, radius 16. Workspace chip on top, nav in the middle, account row pinned to the bottom. Items are 34px, radius 9, icon 14px + label 13px.
Selection motion
One white pill, translated to the active item — the only element that moves. It glides (180ms, house curve); text weight and colour cross-fade under it. Icons never turn green.
Guidelines

The discipline.

Do
Glide the one active pill. Keep icons in ink, never green. Pin account to the bottom. Let the glass float over the working canvas.
Don't
Overshoot or bounce the pill. Animate more than the selection. Colour the icons. Make the rail solid — it's the one glass object.

Variants
Expanded (272) · collapsed (56, labels on hover, a green signal dot on any collapsed group holding the active page). Groups carry uppercase headers with a chevron; items may carry a status pill (“Review”) or a BETA pill.
The eight states
Every interactive component provides all eight or declares which do not apply, with the reason. A silent omission is the bug this table exists to prevent.
Rest Glass card, 272px (56 collapsed). Items 32px at --radius-md, 13px labels in --ink-500, icons 14px — never green.
Hover Non-active items take --hover-on-canvas. The label settles weight and ink on --duration-soft.
Pressed brightness(0.94) from motion-base.
Focus motion-base ring on the item. The rail is a list — arrow keys move within it, Tab leaves it.
Selected ONE white pill (--selected-pill, --shadow-raised) that GLIDES to the selection over --duration-slow on --ease-glide; items never paint their own active fill. Crossing groups, the pill hands off with a --duration-soft fade.
Disabled n/a a destination the client cannot use is not shown. A gated one shows a status pill instead.
Loading The rail renders immediately with real labels; only counts and status pills are Skeletons. Navigation must never wait for data.
Empty A fresh client sees only the fixed destinations — no ghost rows, no “coming soon” entries.
Content rules
Destinations are single nouns — Home · Work queue · Performance · Knowledge · Design · Agents. Never verbs, never “My…”, never a count in the label (counts are a quiet pill on the right). Group headers are one uppercase word. A destination’s name never changes with state.
