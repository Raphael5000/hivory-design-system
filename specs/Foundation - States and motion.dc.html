Design System
Foundation · States & motion

Things settle. Nothing lifts.

Every state change eases softly on one curve at one default duration. Movement is reserved for things that genuinely travel — a selection pill, an arriving toast — and never for feedback.

Motion

Eight durations, two curves.

There are no literal ms or cubic-bezier values anywhere in the system — 120 declarations were converted on 31 July. 150 and 160ms were in use in sixteen places and collapsed into --duration-soft: against 180 they are sub-perceptual, and a distinction nobody can see is entropy rather than craft.

Focus, press & reduced motion

The three rules in every file.

These cannot be written inline — they are pseudo-class and media rules — so they live identically in all 39 surfaces as motion-base.

Focus ring2px --border-focus outline at 2px offset, on :focus-visible only — so a mouse click never draws a ring but a Tab always does. Applied through :where() so it costs no specificity and any component can override it.
Nothing liftsHover is tint, fill or ring — never displacement. No rise, no nudge, no scale: the cursor already says where you are, and a target that moves as you approach it is a worse target. --hover-lift was retired on 31 July (held at 0 so the bundle still resolves).
PressedOne system-wide brightness(0.94) on :active. Chosen over a scale or an opacity shift because it works on a filled button, a ghost link and a tinted chip alike — and because inline styles beat stylesheet rules, so an opacity press would silently lose on every element that sets its own. Disabled controls opt out.
Reduced motionTransitions and entrances collapse to 1ms. But motion that carries information — spinners, pulse dots, the queue's running word, skeleton shimmer — keeps looping at a calmed 2.4s. Removing it would delete the only signal that says work is in flight. Mark it data-motion="essential", or list the selector in the surface's reduced-motion block when the animation comes from a class.
Duration sourceEvery transition takes its duration and easing from a token. 120ms is press-only: on 31 July sixteen state transitions across seven components were found easing at 120ms, which made hover snap where it should settle.

Known gap · keyboard operability

A ring nothing can reach.

277 clickable div and span elements across the system carry an onClick. None is keyboard-reachable — no tabindex, no role, and therefore no focus ring however correct the rule above is.
Deliberately not patched by adding tabindex in bulk. A focus stop that Enter and Space cannot activate is worse than no focus stop: it promises an affordance the element does not have. Real repair is per component — the right element (button where it acts, a where it navigates), the right role, and a key handler in the logic class.
Do
Take every duration and curve from a token. Express hover as tint, fill or ring. Keep informational motion running under reduced motion, calmed. Show focus only to the keyboard.
Don't
Lift, nudge or scale on hover. A literal ms value. 120ms on a state change. Bounce or spring. A spinner where a Skeleton belongs. Stacked thinking lines. A focus ring a mouse can trigger.
