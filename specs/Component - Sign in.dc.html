Do
Put providers above the field. Give the plate one line and let it breathe. Keep the form identical in every deployment. Autofocus the field. Let password managers fill it.
Don't
A screenshot or illustration on the plate. A brand-coloured CTA. A testimonial — nobody reads a quote while signing in. Errors that confirm an account exists. Attempt counters. Real client copy in the system.
