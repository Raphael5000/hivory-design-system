Not a stack of cards. The system already says one table card per view with section heads inside it — so an accordion is that table, where a row can disclose its own breakdown. Ranked list on top, detail one level down, and the row never stops being a row.

Content rules
Head labels are the category as the client would say it, sentence case, no counts in the words — the count has its own column. A row with nothing beneath it still opens, and says so in one plain sentence rather than showing an empty area.
Do
Keep one card and put rows inside it. Rotate the chevron rather than swapping the glyph. Let many rows open at once. Indent the body to the label, not the card edge.
Don't
A card per row. A second meter column. Swapping + for −. A height animation. Nesting a second accordion inside one — two levels is the limit, and a third means the data wants a table of its own.
