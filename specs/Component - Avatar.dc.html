The spec
Two initials at weight 650 on --ink-100 — one wash for everyone, no per-person colors. Photos crop into the same circle. Presence is a 8px green dot with a 2px surface ring, bottom-right, breathing. The workspace tile is the single dark square (radius 8, ink-900) — brands, never people.

The stack
7px overlap, each ringed 2px in the surface it sits on. Four faces at most, then the +N chip on --ink-50 — hover it for the rest. Most recent actor leads.
Guidelines

The discipline.

Do
Two initials, first + last. Tooltip the full name on hover and focus. Presence only where "online now" matters — and only one breathing dot per view. Ring color matches the surface underneath.
Don't
Per-person background colors — the palette belongs to the client's brand. Dark circles for people (dark is the workspace's alone). Stacks past +N. Green rings, badges, or status glyphs on the avatar itself — presence is the one dot.

Variants
Person (round) · company (--radius-sm) · with presence dot · AvatarGroup (overlapping stack). Sizes follow the row that holds them — 20px in tables, 28px in list rows, 32–44px in headers.
The eight states
Every interactive component provides all eight or declares which do not apply, with the reason. A silent omission is the bug this table exists to prevent.
Rest Initials in --ink-700 on --ink-50 at 999px. Company avatars square off to --radius-sm; people stay round — shape carries the kind.
Hover n/a an avatar is an identity, not a control. Hover belongs to the row containing it.
Pressed n/a not interactive on its own; a linked avatar inherits brightness(0.94) from its wrapper.
Focus n/a never takes a tab stop of its own. Focus lands on the row or link around it.
Selected n/a selection is the containing row’s state, never the face’s.
Disabled n/a identity has no disabled state. A deactivated member is said in the row copy, never by greying the face.
Loading Skeleton circle at the same diameter. Never initials of a guessed name — a wrong face is worse than no face.
Empty No name yet: a hairline ring on --surface-card, no glyph. Never a silhouette icon and never a question mark.
Content rules
Initials are one or two letters, uppercase, taken from the display name as given — never derived from an email local part, which produces initials the person does not recognise. Two letters for people; up to three for a company with a known abbreviation. AvatarGroup shows at most four faces then “+N” in --ink-500, and that number is never a link. The presence dot is never captioned — if the state needs words it is a Badge.
