The front door.

The whole product starts here: you ask, the engine goes to work. A white card — not a field — with a green caret, one paperclip, and a Send that waits at half strength until you've said something. Type below.

Thinking — reading memory…
Enter sends, Shift+Enter breaks a line. Focus blooms the card — a soft green halo on --ease-glide. The empty composer rotates real asks. And Send ripens: ink at 45% until the ask exists, then it settles into green-500 — the screen's one brand CTA.
Anatomy

One voice, two homes.

Home — the ask
Centered, radius 18, the screen's one elevated card. 14.5px text on a 56px starting height, request chips beneath offering starts — "Plan a campaign", never commands. Sending routes the ask and opens the session.
Chat — pinned below
Reply to the Writer… Send
The same card compressed to a pill, pinned to the session's bottom edge. Grows from 22px to 140px, then scrolls. One composer per screen, always.
Guidelines

The discipline.

Do
Green caret, always — it's the engine's tell. Gate Send with opacity, never disable it. One short placeholder naming real outcomes. Attachments as chips inside the card, read before anything else. Auto-grow, then scroll at 140px.
Don't
Formatting toolbars, mic or emoji buttons — it's an ask, not a document. Borders or inset fields — the card is the input. Placeholder essays. Two composers on one screen. A Send arrow-icon button — the verb is spelled out.

Variants
Home composer (rotating example placeholder, brand CTA) · in-session composer (pinned bottom, same card edge as the content column). Attachment chips sit inside the card and ride the sent bubble as a document chip.
The eight states
Every interactive component provides all eight or declares which do not apply, with the reason. A silent omission is the bug this table exists to prevent.
Rest White pill card at --radius-2xl on --shadow-card, textarea 22px growing to 140px max, Send at 45% ink opacity while empty.
Hover Attach and Send lighten their ink at --duration-soft.
Pressed brightness(0.94) from motion-base.
Focus Focus BLOOMS the card — a soft green halo ring over --duration-gentle. This is the one place a halo is legal, because the composer is the screen’s subject, not a field in a form.
Selected n/a
Disabled n/a the composer is never disabled. A session that cannot take input says so in the thread and the composer explains why.
Loading While the engine works the composer stays live so the next ask can be typed; the THREAD shows the thinking line.
Empty The empty Home composer rotates real example asks as its ghost placeholder on a 3.4s cycle — never “Type a message…”.
Content rules
The placeholder is a real ask in the client’s own words, first person, no ellipsis-as-decoration: “Write next month’s posts about the refill launch”. Send never says “Send” in the label — it is a glyph. Attachment chips say where the file will end up (“will be filed in Knowledge”), because filing is a consequence the client should see before sending.
