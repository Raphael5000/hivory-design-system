In its habitat — the Work-queue table's Channel column. One tone, five glyphs, instantly tellable.
Anatomy

One wash, many glyphs.

The spec
22px pill on a 5% ink wash, label 11.5 at weight 600 in --text-nav, Tabler glyph at 12px leading. Canonical glyphs: brand-linkedin · article · world · filter · mail — a new channel gets a glyph pick recorded in the PDL, never an invented color.
Why no color
The console's palette is spoken for: green is the one signal, amber means needs-you, and everything else belongs to the client's brand. Five colored pills would out-shout all three. The glyph differentiates just as fast — and the table stays quiet.
Guidelines

The discipline.

Do
One chip per row, in the Channel column. Glyph + label always — the glyph alone only where the column header names the channel. Record every new channel's glyph pick. Keep the wash at 5% ink on white surfaces.
Don't
Brand-colored or per-channel hues — differentiation is the glyph's job. Status meanings (that's Badge). Green or amber washes — those belong to state. Chips as filters (the filter row's toggle chips are Tabs pills, a different component).

Variants
Five channels — LinkedIn · Blog · Website · Pipeline · Email — told apart by GLYPH only. Never by hue: colour in the console belongs to state and to the client’s brand.
The eight states
Every interactive component provides all eight or declares which do not apply, with the reason. A silent omission is the bug this table exists to prevent.
Rest 22px pill, --hairline wash, --text-nav label at 11.5/600, Tabler glyph at 12px.
Hover n/a in a table — the ROW carries the hover wash. A chip that hovers inside a hovering row reads as two targets.
Pressed n/a as a label. As a filter toggle it takes brightness(0.94).
Focus n/a as a label. As a filter toggle it takes the motion-base ring.
Selected As a filter only: --action-primary fill with a white label. As a label it has no selected state.
Disabled n/a a channel is a fact about the job, and facts are not disabled.
Loading n/a the row’s Skeleton covers it.
Empty Omitted. Every job has a channel; if one genuinely does not, the cell stays blank rather than saying “None”.
Content rules
One word, the channel as the client says it, capitalised as the platform capitalises itself (“LinkedIn”). Never the internal tool name, never an API name, never a code. If a channel needs two words it is probably two channels.
