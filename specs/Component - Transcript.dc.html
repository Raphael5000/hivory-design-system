Design System
Component · Transcript

Where this came from.

The conversation that produced a job, shown as history. Same anatomy as the live chat, every affordance removed — because the chat IS the permanent comment record, and a record you can edit is not a record.

Anatomy

The live chat, with the verbs taken out.

The left gutter28px, holding the 16px hex mark OUTSIDE the content column — so engine text, artifact cards and the client's own bubbles all share one left edge. Inherited from the live chat unchanged; it is what stops a transcript reading as two ragged columns.
YouInk-5.5% wash bubble, radius 16/16/5/16, right-aligned, max 78%. The sharp corner is the speaker's corner.
The enginePlain text on the canvas at 13.5/1.7 — no bubble, no card. The asymmetry is deliberate: the client's words are quoted, the engine's are simply the page talking.
Steps, collapsed"4 steps · 41s" renders as static text with the chevron at rest. It never expands here — what the engine did belongs to the live session, and a transcript that unfolds becomes a log.
Artifact cards
are inertThey render in full, with their OUTCOME in the sub line ("Approved 24 Jul · published 25 Jul, 14:00") rather than a state. Not clickable: opening a panel from inside a history view would put a workspace inside a record.
One way outA single "Open the session ›" link at the foot — the same one-way-out rule the Mock Kit uses. It goes to the live chat where acting is legitimate. Everything else stays dead.
Where it appears

Three places, one reason.

Work queue jobOpened from a row's provenance. Answers "why does this job exist" without making the client hunt through sessions — the queue is the ledger, and this is the ledger's citation.
Performance chainBehind a shipped outcome: the ask, the drafts, the approval, the result. The chain's credibility comes from being able to walk back to the sentence that started it.
Proposal contextIn read mode, showing a prospect how the loop actually sounds. Nothing is live there anyway, so the read-only contract costs nothing — and the same component means the proposal can never drift from the product.
The eight states

A record has almost none.

RestThe only state. Every message is final; nothing is pending, thinking or in flight.
HoverNothing responds except the one footer link, which behaves as a link. No message hover, no "fix" ghost, no copy affordance — those all belong to editable surfaces.
PressedDoes not apply except on the footer link.
FocusOne tab stop: the footer link. The transcript body is a document — a screen reader walks it as text, which is exactly right for a record.
SelectedDoes not apply. Text selection works because it is prose; there is no selectable message.
DisabledDoes not apply. Nothing is disabled, because nothing was ever enabled — this is the distinction from read mode on an artifact, where affordances exist and are suppressed.
LoadingSkeleton two bubbles and two prose blocks at their real measures, keeping the header real. Never the thinking spinner — that would claim the conversation is still happening.
EmptyA job with no conversation behind it (a standing job's occurrence, a system-initiated run) shows one line: "No request — this ran on the weekly rule" with a link to the rule. Never an empty frame: the absence is itself the provenance.
Content & laws

Verbatim, or it is not a record.

Never summarisedThe client's words appear exactly as typed — no tidying, no truncation with an ellipsis, no "the client requested changes to the tone". The whole value of the transcript is that it is not a paraphrase.
No composer,
everNot even disabled. A greyed input invites typing and then refuses it; its absence says plainly that this is somewhere else's conversation. The footer link is how you get to a place you can talk.
Outcomes, not statesArtifact sub lines read "published 25 Jul, 14:00", never "Held for you". A live state inside a historical view is a lie about the present, and it will be wrong within a day.
Dates follow
the standardSentence contexts may say "Today" or "2 h ago"; the header and outcome lines take the date form (24 Jul). One clock everywhere, per the time standard.
It is not a logNo per-message timestamps, no agent names in the flow, no expanded steps, no tool calls. Those are ops chrome; the client sees the conversation they had, not the machinery that served it.
Do
Quote the client verbatim. Keep the left gutter so everything shares one edge. Render artifact cards with their outcome. Offer exactly one way out, at the foot. Say plainly that it is read-only.
Don't
A disabled composer. Expandable steps. Clickable artifact cards. Live states in sub lines. Per-message timestamps. Summarised or truncated client words. A thinking spinner. Agent names in the flow.
Tokens used

Every value, named.

Colourrgba(23,24,26,0.055) the client bubble wash (the live chat's own value) · --text-body both speakers · --green-600 the hex mark, per the mark-usage rule for functional inline marks · --text-muted steps and sub lines · --text-faint the date and the read-only note · --surface-card + --shadow-card artifact cards.
MotionNone. A record does not animate — no entrance, no stagger, no fade. The live chat's arrival motion belongs to messages arriving; here they have all already arrived.
GeometryBubble radius 16/16/5/16, max-width 78%, gutter 28px, artifact card --radius-lg. Rhythm: 20 between messages (Block), 12 message→artifact and above the footer (Sibling), 8 to the steps line (Pair).
