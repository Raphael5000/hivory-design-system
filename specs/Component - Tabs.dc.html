Anatomy
28px pill, 13px at weight 550, 6px gap between pills. Rest wears the hairline ring; selected trades it for the ink fill — one selected pill per row, always. Counts ride inside as a 10.5px mono-numeral pill that inverts with its tab.
Guidelines

The discipline.

Do
One selected pill per row. Settle selection in tint, crossfade the panel. Counts only where the number carries meaning (queue sizes). Text tabs inside cards; pill tabs everywhere else. Arrow keys move focus along the row.
Don't
Underline tabs or sliding indicators — movement belongs to the sidebar. Green fills for selection — green is a signal, not a state. More than six tabs (use a Menu). Tabs for actions — a tab shows, a button does.

Variants
With counts · without counts. One row only — no nested tabs, no vertical tabs, no scrolling tab strips: more than about five tabs means the content wants a rail, not a strip.
The eight states
Every interactive component provides all eight or declares which do not apply, with the reason. A silent omission is the bug this table exists to prevent.
Rest Quiet underline row; inactive labels in --ink-700 with a count pill in --ink-100.
Hover Label goes --ink-950; the underline does not appear until selection.
Pressed brightness(0.94) from motion-base.
Focus 2px --border-focus ring on the tab. Arrow keys move between tabs; Tab moves into the panel — the standard tablist contract.
Selected Label --ink-950 at weight 600 with the ink underline, and the count pill turns --green-50 / --green-800. This is the one component where selected and hover must be told apart at a glance.
Disabled Label at opacity .45, still announced. A tab with no content yet is disabled, never removed — removing it changes the shape of the page.
Loading The tab row renders immediately; the PANEL loads. Never hold the tabs back for their content.
Empty A tab whose panel is empty still shows, and the panel carries the empty sentence. Hiding the tab hides the fact that the thing exists.
Content rules
One or two words, sentence case, parallel in kind — all nouns or all states, never mixed (“Companies · People · Deals”, not “Companies · Recently added”). Counts are quiet and never coloured except when selected. The first tab is the one a client wants most often, not the alphabetically first.
