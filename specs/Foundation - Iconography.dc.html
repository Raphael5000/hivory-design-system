Design System
Foundation · Iconography

One line, everywhere.

Tabler is the system set — outline only, round caps, drawn on a 24 grid and worn at 14, 16, or 18. Icons speak in the ink ladder and never in green. The hexagon stays ours.

Scope

Platform only.

Tabler governs the console, the proposal portal and every component page. It does not govern the marketing site or the Marketing kit — those carry their own editorial glyphs at the site's own weight, drawn to sit beside Martel rather than beside 13px rows. A Tabler icon on hivory.io is the same category error as a serif heading in the Work queue.
Permanently exempt
The Mock Kit's bodies — their glyphs imitate the surface they depict, so a Tabler icon there would break the fidelity the kit exists for. And the brand marks: the dual-hex Spinner, the hex agent mark, the Hivory lockup. Everything else in the platform is Tabler, and a hand-drawn stroke SVG on a platform surface is debt.
The house checkmarkThe 9–11px check inside pills, nodes and steppers (M3 8.5 6.5 12 13 4.5) stays hand-drawn. Tabler’s ti-check is optically heavier at that size and is only used at 14px and above — so the small check is a drawn glyph by design, not debt.
Chart geometrySeries lines, area fills, sparklines and meter tracks are DATA, not icons. They are drawn from values and have no Tabler equivalent by definition.
Rules

Worn quietly, in ink.

Sizes & color
14 dense 16 default 18 roomy active disabled
Sized by font-size, colored by the ink ladder, transitioned on --duration-soft. An icon is never green — green belongs to signals, not symbols.
The brand exceptions
Three glyphs stay hand-drawn and are never replaced by Tabler: the dual-hex Spinner, the hex agent, and the Hivory mark itself. The hexagon is the brand's signature — Tabler covers everything else.
Guidelines

The discipline.

Do
One concept, one glyph — record the pick so it never drifts. Outline style only. Pair icon-only controls with a Tooltip and an aria-label. 14px in the sidebar and dense rows, 16 as the default.
Don't
Filled or duotone variants. Green icons, badge dots baked into glyphs, or emoji as icons. Text characters posing as icons (✕ ▲ ⤢) — every glyph comes from the set. Mixing Tabler with leftover hand-drawn strokes on the same surface.
