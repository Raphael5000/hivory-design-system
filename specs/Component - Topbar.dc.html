Whose data am I looking at?

The console has no topbar — its rail carries identity, because there is only ever one client. A reseller portal serves many, so it needs a bar whose entire job is answering that question and letting you switch. It holds identity and account, never navigation — and it floats on the canvas as glass, the rail’s treatment turned horizontal.

Content rules
The client name is the client's own name, never a code. The crumb is one level and names the screen, not the path. The sync line is a sentence a human would say — "synced 12 minutes ago", never a timestamp. Avatar initials come from the display name, never an email.
Do
Put the client name where the eye lands first. Let the sync line degrade to a caution pill when it fails. Keep it to one row at 52px. Drop the crumb and sync line before the identity on small screens.
Don't
Navigation — that is the rail's job, and a bar with tabs makes two navigations. A second row. A notification bell. Unread badges. A brand-coloured bar. The page title, which belongs to the page.
