The grammar

Bars are controls.

Selection, not decoration
Every bar selects its phase; the detail panel below is the single source of names, dates and deliverables. Rest bars are a 7% ink wash with ink-600 labels; the selected bar fills ink-950 with a soft green-tinted shadow. No separate tab row — the bars ARE the tabs. Labels never truncate: inside the bar at 3+ weeks, outside to its right below that (bold ink when selected).
Time & the detail panel
Weeks (W1…W8) until a contract is signed — the gantt promises sequence and pace, not calendar dates; switch to months past 8 weeks. The detail panel carries one description paragraph and a KEY DELIVERABLES 2-column checklist (green checks, ink-3.5% rows, 4 items max). Green appears only in checks and the caps label — never as a bar fill.
Guidelines

The discipline.

Do
Four to five phases, staircase bottom-up — first phase bottom-left so the eye climbs with time. Client-language names ("First drafts, held", never "sprint 2"). A guideline caveat under the card. Compress the lane pitch before growing the card — whitespace must carry sequence, not padding.
Don't
A color per phase — ink wash + ink-950 selected is the whole palette. Dependency arrows, percent-complete, avatars. Mid-word ellipsis on bar labels. A duplicate tab row. Calendar dates before signature. Horizontal scroll — compress the window instead.
