Gap 4 · White label

Right instinct, wrong lever.

whoyou-theme.css already applies a [data-brand="whoyou"] scope, keeps the ink neutrals, and — impressively — keeps a separate semantic green for success so verification “resolved” does not become brand cyan. That is exactly the law the White label page landed on independently this morning, which is strong evidence the rule is correct.
The one real problem
It rebrands by overwriting the whole --green-* ramp with cyan. After that, --green-500 is not green, and every rule reading it lies about what it does. It also drags along everything green touches that ISN'T brand — so the theme has to hand-patch --status-positive-* back with two raw hex values to undo its own override. The DS answer is to swap the three brand tokens only (--action-brand, --on-action-brand, --border-focus) and leave the ramp alone — then status needs no patching, because nothing overwrote it.
Verdict

Four days, not a rebuild.

The real finding
This portal is the DS's first outside consumer, and it drifted because it was copied rather than linked. Nothing warned it that v3 became v4. If white-labelling is a real product line, the fix is not this audit — it is shipping the tokens as a versioned package the portal imports, so drift becomes an upgrade rather than an archaeology exercise. That is the gap behind all four gaps above.
