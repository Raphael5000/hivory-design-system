Anatomy

One chip, one job.

Approve RegenerateR
The spec
Ink chip on --surface-dark, 11.5px at weight 550, radius 8, 6×10 padding, 8px above its target, centered. Optional mono shortcut hint at 55% white. No arrow — the chip floats. Shows on hover and keyboard focus; the target keeps a matching aria-label.
Guidelines

The discipline.

Do
Name icon-only controls and truncated text — nothing else. Two or three words, verb first. One intent delay in, instant out. Show on focus as well as hover. Shortcut hints in mono when the shortcut exists.
Don't
Sentences, links, or buttons inside — a tooltip is a label, not a popover. Tooltips on things that already say their name. Critical information only a hover can find. Arrows, springs, or instant flicker.
Tokens used
6 tokens, read from the component's stylesheet. This is the contract: change one of these and the component changes everywhere.
Ink --ink-950--white
Geometry --radius-sm
Motion --duration-fast--ease-out
Type --font-sans
No surface token: the chip is --ink-950 on white text, which is why it reads as an object rather than a panel. It carries no --surface-* and no shadow beyond the overlay.
Variants
Positions: top · bottom · left · right, flipping to stay inside the viewport. One geometry, one tone — there is no light tooltip and no coloured tooltip.
The eight states
Every interactive component provides all eight or declares which do not apply, with the reason. A silent omission is the bug this table exists to prevent.
Rest --ink-950 chip with white text at --radius-sm, appearing after a short delay on --duration-soft.
Hover This IS the rest state — a tooltip exists only on hover, which is why hover cannot also be a variation of it.
Pressed n/a nothing inside is pressable. A tooltip containing a control is a popover.
Focus Shows on :focus-visible of its trigger as well as on hover — otherwise the label is unreachable by keyboard, which is the most common tooltip failure.
Selected n/a
Disabled A disabled trigger still shows its tooltip; that is often the only place the REASON can live.
Loading n/a
Empty n/a no label means no tooltip.
Content rules
A short label, not a sentence: two to six words, sentence case, no full stop. It names the thing or its shortcut — never repeats visible text, and never carries information available nowhere else. If it needs a second line it is not a tooltip.
