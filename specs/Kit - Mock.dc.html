The work, as it will exist.

Inside every artifact body sits a mock: a still of the surface the work will land on. Five of them carry almost everything the engine makes. They are drawn to the anatomy of the real thing and to nobody's brand but the client's.

record tableThis depicts the destination, never our own review surface. mock/crm is a still of HubSpot showing where records will LAND — six rows, truncated, read-only, with a rail because that is how a CRM is shaped. The console's prospect-delivery table is a different object: Hivory's own Table for approving a batch, which follows Table's grammar (tabs, filters, our columns) and not this. Matching them would make our review surface pretend to be someone else's product.

paletteThe client's colours, never ours. A page mock in Hivory green is the single most common mistake in this kit — it makes our brand look like their site. Pull the palette from Design.

channel picks
the mockart/sequence is not email-only. Its payload carries a CHANNEL per step, and the step renders the mock that matches: mock/email for mail, mock/direct-message for social. One body, two mocks — consistent with the generative contract, where the agent emits a typed payload and the panel chooses the renderer. A sequence that mixes channels shows each step in its own surface, which is the honest picture of what the prospect will receive.

The laws

A mock is a still, not a screen.

Anatomy, not skinFidelity lives in proportion and hierarchy — avatar size, measure, the order of information. We draw no host platform's logo, wordmark or brand colour. A mock that needs someone else's logo to be recognisable is badly drawn.
The client is the only colourMock chrome is grayscale. The colour inside comes from the client's identity in Design — their mark, their accent, their imagery. Hivory green never appears in a mock except on a merge field.
Preview, never liveEvery affordance the real surface would offer is drawn at --text-ghost and does nothing. Nothing in a mock is hoverable, clickable or scrollable — except the image slot, which is the one place the client's own file goes in.
No invented numbersFollower counts, likes, open rates, deal values: shown only when they come from a connected account, otherwise omitted with one honest line ("Reactions appear once this is live"). Fake engagement in a mockup is a lie the client will remember. Every real figure carries tabular-nums — since mono was retired, that is what marks a machine fact.
Say where it came fromRecords carry a source and a sync age. Personalised copy carries the evidence it was written from. Sequences carry their sending rules. The provenance line is part of the anatomy, not a caption.
Truncate honestlySix rows then "6 of 118". Three lines then "…see more". A fade rather than a hard stop. The mock proves the shape; the full thing lives in Work.
Content reflows,
layout scalesA post, a message, an email, a record table are documents — they reflow to the width they are given, up to a measure cap, and their type size never changes. A website is a layout — drawn once at 1000px and scaled as a miniature, so it stays the design it actually is. Reflowing a landing page turns it into a different landing page; scaling an email turns it into unreadable type.
Shed, don't shrinkWhen a document mock loses width it drops columns and folds rails — the CRM's object rail becomes a count strip, Buyer and Stage fall away. Row heights, avatars and type sizes hold at every width. A mock that gets denser as it gets narrower is unreadable at exactly the moment it matters.
One way outA mock may carry exactly one live control, and it lives in the chrome, never in the content: View website, Open in HubSpot. It goes to the real thing in a new tab and does nothing else — it never approves, edits or changes state. Its label collapses to the glyph at compact width. Everything inside the mock stays inert.
Mode-blindA mock never knows whether it sits in the console or a proposal. Read mode mounts the same markup with edit affordances off. If a surface seems to need a different mock, it needs a different payload.
Do
Draw the surface at its real measure and let the artifact frame scale it. Set machine facts — URLs, addresses, counts, filenames — in tabular-nums and a quieter colour, and leave what a human wrote in normal figures. Give every mock one honest line about what it is not yet. Fill the copy with the client's actual voice; a mock with lorem in it has not been designed.
Don't
Host logos or host brand colours. Invented likes, opens or follower counts. Hivory green inside the client's page. Drop shadows or radii on full-bleed media. A second bubble to imply a conversation. Tabs, scrollbars or anything that suggests the mock is operable. Screenshots — a mock is drawn, so it stays sharp and stays editable.
