The other half of the brand.

hivory.io is a separate language from the console, and deliberately so: warm ivory rather than cool grey, Martel serif rather than the system face, editorial rhythm rather than dense rows. They share a logo, a green and an ink ladder — nothing else. Read from the live site on 31 July; the look is correct and this kit exists to preserve it while the product works its way in.

The laws

Bringing the product in.

The site stays airyProduct enters slowly and never at the cost of the calm. One artifact per plate, generous margin, nothing tiled. If a section needs three screenshots to make its point, the point is wrong.
Show the WORK,
not the appThe proof is what the engine made \u2014 a post, a page, a buyer list \u2014 rendered through the Mock Kit at the site's own scale. A console screenshot shows chrome the visitor has no context for; an artifact shows the thing they would receive.
No browser frames,
no device bezelsThe live site never wraps anything in fake chrome and must not start. A product plate is a white card on ivory with a real shadow \u2014 the same restraint the console uses, which is the one place the two languages agree.
Serif claims,
sans evidenceMartel carries the claim; the system face carries the proof. A number, a caption or anything inside a product plate is sans \u2014 the serif is the agency talking, and product surfaces are not the agency talking.
One accent hairline
per sectionThe 96px green rule that grows from the left is the site's punctuation. It opens a section and never appears twice in one; more than one turns a signature into decoration.
Reveal, never animateContent fades up 30px once, on scroll, at 600\u2013800ms on the reveal curve with a 60ms stagger, and then holds still. Nothing loops, nothing parallaxes. Reduced motion shows everything immediately \u2014 the live site already does this and it is not negotiable.
Dark is for the bandThe layout goes dark exactly once per page, at the CTA. The site also ships a full dark mode (accent drops to #00B25D) \u2014 that is a user preference, not a design device, and no section may go dark on its own to look important.
Do
Keep the ivory. Let sections breathe at 96\u2013128px. Put the claim in Martel and the evidence in sans. Use the editorial row for any list of things. Let the product appear as one calm object.
Don't
Glass Engine surfaces on the site, or ivory in the console. Martel below H2 or in product plates. A screenshot collage. Cool grey anywhere. Console density \u2014 13px rows belong to the tool, not the page selling it.
