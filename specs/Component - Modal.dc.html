Design System
Component · Modal

Are you sure?

The one place we stop the user — a double confirmation, never an inline warning or a browser dialog. The title is a question, the body is one plain sentence of consequence, and the verb button names the real outcome.

Fades in over a 28% ink scrim (140ms), the panel settling from 98%. Scrim-click, Cancel or Esc all dismiss.
Anatomy & guidelines

The one interruption.

Anatomy
380px panel, radius 16, on a 28% ink scrim. Title = verb + object + "?" (15/650). One consequence sentence (12.5, secondary). Footer right-aligned: Cancel (outline) then the verb — red fill when destructive, ink otherwise.
Don't
Use it for anything but a real double-confirm. Stack modals. Use a browser alert. Write vague titles ("Are you sure?") or bury the consequence.

The bundle component carries no motion tokens and no scrim, though the confirmation doctrine specifies a 140ms fade over a 28% ink scrim — the behaviour this page demonstrates lives in the page, not in the component. It should consume --duration-modal, --ease-out and --scrim, and radius should be --radius-2xl (16) to match the specimen. Logged as the widest DS-versus-doctrine gap found in this pass.
Variants
Confirmation only. One panel width (380), one scrim, one footer shape: Cancel (outline) then the verb (danger fill if destructive, ink otherwise). There is no large modal, no multi-step modal and no modal that scrolls — those are screens.
The eight states
Every interactive component provides all eight or declares which do not apply, with the reason. A silent omission is the bug this table exists to prevent.
Rest 380px panel at --radius-2xl on --surface-card, over a --scrim at 28% ink. Fades in over --duration-modal with the panel settling from 98%.
Hover n/a the panel is a surface; its footer buttons carry hover.
Pressed n/a belongs to the footer buttons.
Focus Focus moves INTO the panel on open and is trapped there; Esc closes and returns it to the trigger. The first focus stop is Cancel, never the destructive verb.
Selected n/a
Disabled n/a a modal is either open or absent. A disabled modal is a screen that should not have interrupted.
Loading After the verb is pressed the verb button loads in place; the panel does not close until the work resolves or fails.
Empty n/a a modal with nothing to say must not open.
Content rules
Title is the verb plus its object plus a question mark: “Delete this template?”. One sentence of consequence in plain language, present tense, saying what happens and what survives: “Its extracted facts are withdrawn from memory. The original file stays stored.” The confirm button repeats the title’s verb — never “Yes”, never “OK”. Never ask a question the user cannot answer from the panel alone.
