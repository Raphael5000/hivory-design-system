Anatomy
Trigger: 26–28px pill, label + chevron; active = ink fill with the count in the label ("Status · 2") — never a badge stuck on top. Menu: DS Menu chrome, 32px checkbox rows (15px box, radius 4, ink fill + white check), optional status tone dot right-aligned, "Clear" ghost row below a hairline once anything is picked.
Guidelines

The discipline.

Do
One trigger per dimension (Status, Channel), right of the search field. Tally selections into the trigger label. Keep the menu open across picks. Pair with an "N of M" count + ✕ reset beside the search. Ink checks — green stays a signal, not a control.
Don't
Lay options out as a row of pills — over ~3 options that's noise, use this instead. Close the menu on every check. Use green fills for checked states. Stack more than three filter dimensions on one ledger.

Variants
One Filter Select per dimension (Status · Channel), placed right of the search field. Never a row of option pills — that spends the whole width on choices nobody has made yet.
The eight states
Every interactive component provides all eight or declares which do not apply, with the reason. A silent omission is the bug this table exists to prevent.
Rest Quiet pill trigger at --radius-pill with a chevron; the panel is Menu chrome at --radius-xl on --shadow-overlay.
Hover Trigger takes --hover-on-white; rows take --ink-50.
Pressed brightness(0.94) from motion-base.
Focus motion-base ring on the trigger and on each checkbox row. Space toggles without closing the panel.
Selected Active filter = --action-primary fill on the trigger reading “Name · N”. The panel STAYS OPEN across picks — closing after each one makes multi-select unusable.
Disabled n/a a dimension with nothing to filter is not rendered.
Loading n/a options are known before the trigger appears.
Empty A --text-faint row saying no options match, plus the Clear row. The Clear row is always present once anything is picked.
Content rules
The trigger says the dimension, not the selection, until something is picked: “Status” then “Status · 2”. Rows are the values as the client reads them elsewhere in the product — identical wording to the Badge and the section header. “Clear”, never “Reset” or “Remove all”.
