Kit law: one chart per card · EVERY chart element answers on hover — lines, bars, compare series (one chip, both values), hbar tracks and sparkline cards alike: ink chip (10px context over 11px mono value), hairline guide + ring dot where there is a series, 120ms ease, never persisting · rows and bars tint on hover · Skeleton shimmer for first paint (the dual-hex Spinner is for actions in flight, never charts) · comparison = previous period in ink dashed 1.5px, no fill, no dot · series 1 is always green-600 on light / green-500 on dark, series 2–4 come from the token ramp (teal · amber · ink), 2 series max in generated views · legend only at ≥2 series (dot + 11px label, top-right) — one series means the header IS the legend · deltas green-700 up / var(--red-700) down, inverted for cost metrics · every value mono tabular, abbreviated (18.2K · $41K · 1.8×) · axis dates follow the platform time standard · empty means "Not enough data — connect <source>", never invented numbers. Banned forever: pies, donuts, gauges, radars, dual axes, 3D, vertical grid lines, y-axis lines, per-chart date pickers.
