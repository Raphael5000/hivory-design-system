Component · Toast

Noted, quietly.

The receipt for a decision already made. An ink notice settles into the bottom-right corner, carries one tone dot and one verb, and leaves on its own. Fire a few below.

Anatomy & tones

One line, one verb.

Approved — publishing now
Held for Thursday 09:00
Publish failed — retrying
Draft saved
The spec
Ink card on --surface-dark, radius 12, 13px at weight 550. One 6px tone dot — green done · amber held · red failed · ink neutral. At most one action verb at 60% white, full white on hover. Bottom-right, 8px stack gap, three visible at most — older receipts collapse first. Hover pauses the clock; failures stay until dismissed.
Guidelines

The discipline.

Do
Confirm what already happened — past tense, one line. Offer the one recovery verb (Undo, Change, Details). Let it leave on its own; keep failures until acted on. Say the consequence: "publishing now", not "success".
Don't
Ask questions in a toast — decisions belong in a Modal. Stack more than three. Slide in from the edge or bounce. White cards, icons, titles, or progress bars — it's a receipt, not a panel. Toast something the user is looking at anyway.

Variants
Tone dots: positive · info · caution · negative. Optional single action (“View ›”). ToastStack holds at most three; a fourth replaces the oldest. Presentational only — the app owns the queue.
The eight states
Every interactive component provides all eight or declares which do not apply, with the reason. A silent omission is the bug this table exists to prevent.
Rest Ink chip (--ink-950), white text at 12px, --radius-lg, bottom-right, with a tone dot. Rises in once over --duration-gentle on --ease-glide.
Hover Auto-dismiss PAUSES while the pointer is over the chip; the action lightens.
Pressed brightness(0.94) on the action and the dismiss.
Focus motion-base ring on the action. A toast never steals focus — it is announced politely, and its action must also exist on the surface it points to.
Selected n/a
Disabled n/a a toast reports something that already happened; there is nothing to disable.
Loading n/a a toast is the RESULT of work. Work in flight belongs to the Spinner or the status strip, never to a toast.
Empty n/a nothing to report means no toast. An empty toast is a bug that shipped.
Content rules
Past tense, one line, naming what happened and where it went: “Deployed — staging.puregood.co is live”, “✓ Added to the Work queue — sending Thu 09:00”. Never “Success!”, never an exclamation mark, never a sentence that wraps. The action is a destination, not a verb (“View ›”, “Work queue ›”). Errors say what to do next and never blame the client.
